## Tested-config facts (NBC spec boxes + measurement sections)
- Display (both): 15.1" 16:10 2560x1600 OLED LEN151WQXGA glossy, 165Hz. **495 nits SDR max (495 avg, 92% dist, min 5.2 nits), 1067 nits HDR small-area / 925 full-screen, ΔE 0.9, 100% sRGB / 99.9% P3 / 98.3% AdobeRGB, infinite contrast, very good viewing angles; PWM flicker listed as con; glossy-only → outdoor reflections "limited usability".** Accurate sRGB & P3 color profiles preinstalled → suited to photo/video editing per NBC.
- Chassis: 1.94 kg (OLED version; IPS is ~200 g heavier + 2.5 mm thicker), 21.5 mm, 344.9x255.4 mm. Aluminum lid, plastic base; "stability of the base unit is very good ... only the center of keyboard area can be pushed down quite easily"; hinges secure, 165° max opening; camera bump; surfaces fingerprint-prone. 734 g / 245 W PSU.
- Battery: 80 Wh. **Wi-Fi 150 nits 60Hz ≈ 9.5 h (AMD), 6.5 h at max brightness, video 11.5 h, ~1 h under load, full charge 95 min (80% in 52).** Intel (14900HX/13650HX) variants: "noticeably shorter battery life" than AMD — estimate ~6.5-7 h WiFi.
- Performance: RTX 5060 and 5070 both at **115 W TGP** (5070: 115W incl 95W Dynamic Boost, Time Spy ~12,306 with 13650HX). Fn+Q modes: Battery Saver 60/50/45W GPU 60W / Auto 75W / Performance 115W. CP2077 QHD 57.9 FPS (5060 config, Performance). CPUs: AMD R7 260 (Zen4 8c, rebranded 8845HS, CB24 multi 1000 / single 106.5, stable loops); Intel i7-13650HX 14c/20t PL2 140W.
- Ports: AMD model: left GbE RJ45 + USB-C 3.2 Gen2 (DP2.1 via dGPU, PD 3.0) + **USB4 40Gbps (DP1.4, via iGPU)** + powered USB-A Gen1; right 3.5mm + 2x USB-A Gen1 + webcam eShutter; rear HDMI 2.1. **Intel model identical but NO USB4** (USB-C Gen2 only). No card reader on any. Wi-Fi 7 MT7925 + BT 5.4 (transmit 1820 MBit/s — very good).
- Inputs: chiclet keyboard w/ numpad, 1.6 mm travel, "very comfortable", 24-zone backlight; clickpad 12x7.8 cm, good glide, shifted left, mechanical clicks "a bit loud".
- Webcam/speakers: 5 MP with eShutter, "quite sharp images, but the color accuracy is not that great"; stereo speakers "could be better".
- Maintenance (NBC: "very good"): 2x M.2 (2242 + 2280), 2x SODIMM DDR5 up to 64 GB, replaceable Wi-Fi module, screwed battery, Lenovo publishes a maintenance manual.
- Emissions: quiet in everyday use ("quiet murmur even in Performance"); 50.1 dB(A) max under gaming/stress — "not overly annoying"; no coil whine on review unit.
