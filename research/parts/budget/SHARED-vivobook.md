## Platform calibration used for ratings
- **Core 5 320**: office-adequate 6-core; UHD iGPU -> gaming ~1.5; ~4.5-5.5h real on the
  small battery; productivity ~6.0 @16GB, ~5.4 @8GB (DDR5 SO-DIMM upgradeable either way).
- **Ryzen 5 3501U / Ryzen 5 40** (same Mendocino 4C/4T Zen 2 class): fine web/office,
  stutters under heavy multitask; Radeon 610M gaming ~1.5; efficient -> ~6-7h;
  productivity ~4.8-5.2 @16GB.
- **Core i3-N305**: 8 E-cores, tolerable multicore for light use (productivity ~4.5),
  very weak UHD GPU (gaming 1.0), UFS storage is the quiet downgrade, Wi-Fi 5 real-world
  irritant; efficient -> ~6h.
- **Snapdragon X1-class**: best battery of the family by far (~10-12h real, silent),
  Copilot+ 45 TOPS NPU -> ai_ml ~4.5; but **8GB RAM ceiling** caps productivity ~5.5 and
  Arm app-translation caveats -> ecosystem ~6.5; Adreno gaming ~2 with compat caveats.
- Panels: 250 nits on Vivobook 15 (dim), 300 nits on Vivobook 16; all ~45% NTSC class ->
  screen_quality 3.6-4.3, readability_bright low.
- Build: all-plastic, lid/deck flex is the recurring family complaint (same direction as
  the IdeaPad Slim 3 calibration: build_quality 4.3-5.0); Go 15 is the flimsiest,
  S16 slightly better finished. Keyboards shallow but consistent, numpad on 15.6" SKUs,
  small Mylar trackpad.
- Upgradability: DDR5/DDR4 SO-DIMM on Vivobook 15/16 = real RAM upgrade path (+);
  LPDDR5/LPDDR5X Go/S16 soldered dead-ends; Go 15 N305 uses non-replaceable UFS (--) .
  M.2 SSD replaceable elsewhere; ASUS CA warranty average.
