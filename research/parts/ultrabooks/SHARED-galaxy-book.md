## Line-level knowledge (disclosed synthesis from prior domain knowledge, NOT fresh quotes)
- **AMOLED is the signature.** Pro tiers ship 3K-class Dynamic AMOLED 120Hz touch panels with true blacks and excellent color — screen_quality ratings for Pro tiers are genuinely high (8.5–9). Base Book6 14 and the 15.6 Book4/Book5 are plain IPS/FHD tiers (~300 nits, 60Hz) — big step down.
- **Galaxy ecosystem is a real differentiator vs other Windows machines:** with a Galaxy phone — Second Screen/Second Phone, Quick Share (AirDrop-grade), Side Sync (phone apps on desktop), Smart Switch, Find My integration, S Pen parity on Pro 360 lines. Ecosystem rating +1 vs generic Windows ultrabooks for Galaxy-phone owners; still Windows x86 = full app compatibility.
- **Copilot+/NPU:** Book6 uses Intel Core Ultra Series 2 (Lunar Lake/Arrow Lake) = 47+ NPU TOPS, qualifies Copilot+. Book5 15.6 (Ultra 7 255U) and Book4 15.6 (Core 7 150U) do NOT qualify — 255U is a low-power office part with a weak NPU; 150U is Raptor Lake refresh, no NPU worth naming.
- **Battery:** Lunar Lake Book6 tiers are the efficiency sweet spot (~12–15h light use realistic). U-series 15.6 machines sip power but carry small batteries (~54–60Wh) → ~8–10h. H-series Book4 Pro (not at BBY) ran hot and throttled in the thin chassis — that's why the H-tier isn't at CA retail anymore.
- **Known user-side complaints across the line (long-standing, from NBC forums/Reddit memory):** soldered RAM everywhere; no SD card reader on non-Pro models; Samsung preinstalled software bloat (Samsung Settings, etc.); Canadian service/parts network thinner than Dell/LP; dim glossy IPS on base tiers; webcam adequate 1080p but processing is flat; coil whine reports on Book4 chargers.
- **Known praises:** thin light chassis (Book6 14 ~1.2 kg class), AMOLED Pro panels, S Pen bundled on 360 Pro models, good quiet keyboards, fast charge, Galaxy phone pairing.
- **Galaxy Book Ultra:** US-only line, not sold in Canada (per OTHER_LIST) — excluded.
