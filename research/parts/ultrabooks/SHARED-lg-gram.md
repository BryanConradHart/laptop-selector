## Line anchor (applies to all gram configs)
LG gram's defining trait: extreme lightness via magnesium-nano-carbon chassis —
every gram model undercuts same-size rivals by 0.5–1 kg. Trade-offs consistently
reported across the line: lid/deck flex tolerated for weight, modest speakers,
soldered RAM, weak North-American service/parts network, glossy touch panels,
thin chassis throttling sustained CPU/GPU power.

## Weights (key differentiator; estimates flagged)
- gram Pro 16 (2025, iGPU): ~1.19 kg — astonishing for 16"
- gram 16 OLED (2024 Meteor Lake): ~1.19 kg
- gram 17 (Lunar Lake AI): ~1.35 kg
- gram Max 17 (2025, numpad): ~1.49 kg (est.)
- gram Book 15.6 / 16 (value line): ~1.65–1.7 kg (est.) — still light for size but not "gram-magic"

## Battery anchor
77–80 Wh in Pro/Max/17; LG claims ~20 h, real-world web use ~9–11 h on Arrow Lake
iGPU models, ~12–14 h on Lunar Lake (best-in-line efficiency), ~7–9 h on the 2024
OLED 155H, ~8–10 h on U-series gram Book (smaller pack, efficient chip).
Calibration note: LG's claimed hours run ~40–50% above real-world; users report
battery ~1–2 h worse than early pro reviews suggested on Arrow Lake models.

## User sentiment themes (synthesis — NOT verbatim quotes; direct threads bot-walled)
Praises (recurring): weight for screen size (top theme, universally), battery life
for real commute/work days, port selection incl. HDMI + microSD despite thinness,
Wi-Fi 7 / Copilot+ on 2025 models, good matte-ish touch IPS panels at 16:10.
Complaints (recurring): lid flex and hinge wobble, weak downward-firing speakers,
soldered RAM/storage limits + no CA self-service parts, LG Vantage software bloat
and erratic driver updates, screen bleeding/glossy reflections, modest sustained
performance from thin chassis, poor retail service coverage in Canada.
