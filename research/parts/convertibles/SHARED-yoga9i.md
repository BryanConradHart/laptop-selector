## Calibration note (users vs pro)
Pro anchor is generation-adjacent (Gen 9 chassis, NBC 90.5%): users at BBY (4.54★/50 on the Gen 11 unit) match the pro score well — the chassis/hinge/speakers/screen universally loved. Users complain MORE than reviews about: (1) price vs integrated graphics, (2) port minimalism on recent gens (USB-C only, dongle life), (3) OLED moire/graininess on white text (NBC noted it too), (4) fan noise + performance drop under sustained load (Mashable flagged noise; users echo thermal throttling in Qt mode). Lunar Lake/Panther Lake tiers fix the Gen 9 battery complaint ("You'll want to make sure you have a charging cable nearby" — Mashable on 155H): Lunar Lake 258V real-world light use ~11–14h, Panther Lake similar or better.
