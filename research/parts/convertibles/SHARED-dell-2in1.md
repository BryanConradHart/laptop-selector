URL: https://www.cnet.com/tech/computing/dell-16-plus-2-in-1-review-the-mini-led-display-shines-but-theres-little-else-to-get-excited-about/
As-tested config: Core Ultra 7 258V, 32GB LPDDR5X, Arc 140V, 1TB SSD, 16" 2560x1600 90Hz **mini-LED** touch, 64Wh, 2.0 kg (4.4-4.5 lb), 0.7" thick, Wi-Fi 7/BT 5.4, TB4 x1 + USB-C 3.2 G2 + USB-A + HDMI 2.1 + audio (no SD, no Kensington), fingerprint power button, 1080p webcam (no IR), price as reviewed $1,549 (frequently $1,250; from $1,450).
Verdict/title quote: "The Mini-LED Display Shines but There's Little Else to Get Excited About" ... "this big-screen convertible laptop certainly has some positives but there are a few too many minuses to give this Plus a full-throated recommendation."
Pros (verbatim): "Bright, striking mini-LED display"; "Strong productivity performance"; "Sturdy chassis".
Cons (verbatim): "Poor battery life"; "Subpar speakers"; "Mushy keyboard".
Key measured facts:
- Battery: "lasted just less than 9 hours on our battery rundown test" (8:58 streaming; class-worst vs Yoga 7 16 at 10:57) — 64Wh + mini-LED penalty.
- Display: mini-LED "Clocking in at 467 nits in my testing, it's a great choice for outdoor or bright indoor workspaces"; lacks OLED blacks/contrast; "really shines in tablet mode".
- Keyboard: "feedback is minimal with mushy keys and very little travel. The spacebar is the worst offender and frequently failed to register my inputs"; full numpad praised; trackpad "lacks haptics... surface feels a bit grainy".
- Speakers down-firing after redesign: "a bit hollow and muffled, as though audio was being broadcast from inside a cardboard box".
- Performance: GB6 single 2760 / multi 11080; Cinebench 2024 122/491; PCMark 6805; 3DMark Time Spy 4190 (Arc 140V). Gaming: "even on low settings, the Dell 16 Plus struggled to hit 25 fps" in AC Shadows / Black Myth.
- Weight: "heavier than other 16-inch two-in-ones" (Spectre x360 16 = 4.3 lb, Yoga 7 16 = 4.4 lb).
- CNET names Lenovo Yoga 7 14 Gen 9 and HP Spectre x360 16 as better competition.
- Two display options: 2.5K mini-LED or **1920x1200 IPS** — CNET: the 16" 1200p IPS "sounds like a recipe for fuzzy, grainy text". CA BBY retail units are the WUXGA/1200p IPS ones (per product names) → downgrade screen_quality vs anchor.
