## User-review findings (Reddit r/SamsungGalaxyTabS11 / r/tablets, BBY reviews — synthesis, disclosed; live threads not fetchable this session)
Praises: S Pen in the box (iPad Pro owners' #1 envy point); DeX with a monitor + BT keyboard/mouse is the closest anything gets to a desktop session on Android; Samsung phone↔table parity (Quick Share, phone-on-PC-style continuity, common clipboard, calls/texts) is genuinely Apple-continuity-grade within the Galaxy family; 144Hz AMOLED; microSD slot still present; 45W charging; 5.1mm Ultra is absurdly thin for 14.6".
