## User-review findings (Reddit r/ipad, BBY reviews — synthesis, disclosed; calibrated with SHARED-ipad-pro.md)
Praises: M4 is overkill-fast for tablet tasks and video edits in LumaFusion/DaVinci iPad; 12GB RAM means no app reloads; function-row Magic Keyboard is more usable than the old Air keyboard; 13" at 616g is the best big-screen/portability trade in the lineup; Wi-Fi 7 + 6K external display support via USB-C works well in Stage Manager.
